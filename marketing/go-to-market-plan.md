# Wayfinder AI — Go-To-Market (GTM) Strategy & Commercial Launch Plan

## 1. Product Positioning & Value Proposition

### One-Sentence Hook
> **"Wayfinder AI is the ambient, proactive browser copilot that reads between the lines of any website—spotting hidden traps, comparing deals, and guiding your decisions before you even have to ask."**

### Core Pillars
1. **Zero-Prompt Proactivity**: Unlike ChatGPT or Claude sidebars where users have to copy-paste and prompt, Wayfinder automatically analyzes the DOM on load and highlights elements with purple callouts.
2. **Privacy First & Zero-PII**: Passwords, credit cards, and sensitive form inputs are blinded locally before any AI inference.
3. **Turnkey Subscription or BYOK**: Users can pay $9.99/mo for zero setup, or plug in their own OpenAI/Claude/Ollama keys for free.

---

## 2. Target Audience Personas

### Persona A: The Smart Online Shopper ("Deal Hunter")
* **Pain Points**: Fake reviews, hidden shipping/return fees, artificial scarcity timers ("Only 2 left!"), price markups disguised as discounts.
* **Wayfinder Value**: Automatic price trap detection, gotcha alert chips, cross-site price comparison in the background.

### Persona B: The High-Frequency Traveler ("Booking Optimizer")
* **Pain Points**: Airline basic economy traps (carry-on bag fees, unassigned seats, tight layovers), vacation rental hidden cleaning fees.
* **Wayfinder Value**: Instantly flags flight traps, warns about tight international connections, reveals neighborhood transit safety.

### Persona C: The Knowledge Worker & Researcher ("Docusign / SaaS Evaluator")
* **Pain Points**: Buried terms in 20-page legal contracts, confusing SaaS pricing matrices, ambiguous opt-out checkboxes.
* **Wayfinder Value**: One-click text selection copilot, contract gotcha extraction, clear plain-English summaries.

## 6. Retention & Anti-Churn Strategy

1. **Weekly Browsing Savings Report**:
   * Extension displays monthly metrics: *"Wayfinder caught 7 gotchas and saved you an estimated $140 this month."*
2. **Instant Grace Period & Downgrade to BYOK**:
   * If a Stripe payment fails or subscription lapses, user is gracefully transitioned to BYOK (Bring Your Own Key) without losing their bookmarks, history, or preferences.
3. **One-Click Stripe Customer Portal**:
   * Transparent subscription management reduces chargebacks and builds long-term customer trust.
